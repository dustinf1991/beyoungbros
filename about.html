About Us

I am Farrah Fisher the owner and art teacher at Be Young Art Club. My family and I love to travel and be in nature. With working backgrounds in both childcare and interior design I was immediately drawn to Reggio Emilia philosophies when thinking about my children's play, art, and education. After years of providing sensory and process art opportunities for my own children I was inspired to start an art club after seeing the lack of true opportunities to create art, be messy, and dive in to the process of art and not just create for a result. There Be Young Art Club was born in our home art studio. I love sharing our space and showing kids all the different ways to create.
