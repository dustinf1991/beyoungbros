About Us

Welcome to our site. We are very excited to have you here! This class started as a small way for a few homeschool students to get together and learn about art and the many different ways to express ourselves through art. We have grown over the years and now have a full class of students from many different homeschool groups. We are so excited to share our projects with you and hope you will join us in the future!
