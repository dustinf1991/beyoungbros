About Us

Welcome to our About Us page! We are excited that you are here and feel it is only fair to give you a little background on us and how we got here! My name is Farrah Fisher and I am the founder of BeYoungBro's. I started this program in 2020 after being inspired by my own children and their friends. I wanted to create a space where kids could come together to learn, create, and grow in a supportive environment. Our mission at BeYoungBro's is to empower young people to explore their creativity and develop new skills through hands-on projects and collaborative learning. We believe that every child has the potential to be a creator, and we are committed to providing the resources and support they need to succeed.
